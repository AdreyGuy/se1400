My journey in CGI

Welcome, everybody! Thanks for visiting my portfolio web page. My name is Andrei Romanchenko, or just Andrew for you. My journey in CGI started when I was 13 years old. Just like every other friend around me back in Russia, we were very passionate about playing good 3-dimensional video games. For example, GTA 5 and GTA SAMP were my absolute favorite.

One year later, I would start creating my own SAMP server. I knew absolutely nothing about developing game modifications, so I would use my pocket money to have teenagers like me build it for me. One more year passed, and it evolved into something bigger than just an innocent hosting server for fun. I convinced my dad to invest in our business, which would collect money from in-game purchases by players. Along that long investment and development process, I've met bad freelancers who would get their job done poorly, so I occasionally had to step in and learn more about certain game development topics myself to save up the budget in the future. Accordingly, the first one that caught my attention was 3d modeling. Knower School was my first online school, which taught me a lot about using 3ds max.

Essentially, I could get some simple objects done for myself and others, including the big project, Twin Falls High School's Stadium, which I was sad about getting over with, as the school's interest in it slowly declined over time and turned into an unfinished and forgotten concept. Yet, I still benefited from it by learning how to operate a DJI drone to gather references, work with textures and UV maps, and model terrains during high school class hours within the school's facility and academic program. That's when I realized that I want to study Animation in college in the United States.

From 2022 to 2023, during my senior year at Bonneville High School, I took my first online character animation course through CG Spectrum. Afterward, I studied for one year at the University of Idaho, pursuing a major in Animation and a minor in Computer Science. I then transferred to Utah Tech University. To this day, I am double-majoring in Software Engineering and Animation while working as a 3D Animator/Artist here at the university in Saint George, Utah.

Andrei Romanchenko
